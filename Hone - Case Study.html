Chapter / 0101
概要the brief

twelve seats. one method. five beans.

Ren opened hone in late 2023 after eight years in tokyo branding studios and a year tasting his way through ethiopia and colombia. the shop is small on purpose: one wooden bar, one brewing method, five rotating beans, never more.

“make it feel like the cafe. no filler, no shouty copy. quiet confidence — the kind of place people come back to because nothing else changed.” — Ren Takahashi, founder

the homepage had to do four things in order: sell beans, fill weekday classes, hold the uncluttered tone of the shop, and grow a global newsletter ahead of international shipping.

the constraint that shaped everything: hone never serves milk. no latte art photography. no foamy hero. the visual story is hands, beans, steam, and a wooden bar.

Chapter / 0303
同一性identity

a kanji, a wordmark, and nothing else.

the mark is a single character. 骨 — read “hone” — means bone, or, more loosely, essence. it is what the shop is named for and what every decision is measured against.

primary / mark
骨
hone
京都の珈琲店

one character, seven strokes, built for type that won't move for the next ten years.

set in shippori mincho — a 21st-century revival of pre-war japanese book typefaces — paired with instrument serif for the latin wordmark. the two typefaces share a calm vertical contrast and feel as though they were always meant to sit together.

no monogram, no badge, no secondary mark. the shop is the brand. the brand is the kanji.

Chapter / 0404
サイトthe homepage

desktop-first. uncrowded. built to live ten years.

a single long page. nav sticks. open status is live. every section earns its place — the bean, the five, the subscription, the classes, the visit, the letter. each frame below is the running site, not a mockup.

hone.kyoto
04 · 01 / hero

one method. five beans. twelve seats.

the entire promise of the shop in three lines. a tategaki rail on the left in mincho — 京都 · 鴨川東岸 · 二〇二三 — gives the page a quiet kyoto-book feel before the user has read a word in english.

the photo is hands at the bar — never milk drinks. the live open / closed pip next to the wordmark uses a soft green pulse, never red.

the headline does the brief — quiet, specific, bilingual. nothing is sold above the fold.
04 · 02 / now pouring

one bean, this week. a 5-line recipe.

process, varietal, roast date, tasting notes, brew ratio. the spec table reads like a label, not marketing — it's how Ren talks to other coffee people.

the accent colour is set by the week's bean: copper this week, deep green for colombia, indigo for indonesia. the dot, the headline italic, the cta — all follow.

a single bean takes up half the page on launch week. specificity is the brand.
hone.kyoto / #beans
hone.kyoto / #five
04 · 03 / the five

five beans, rotating. never more.

a hairline table, no cards, no badges. click any row — it expands inline with brew specs, roast date, and a buy button. the bean that's currently on the bar carries the copper dot and accent.

the row that's "resting" carries a disabled cta with its return date. the constraint is the feature.

a constraint shown plainly beats a benefit invented. listing what's gone is part of the story.
04 · 04 / subscriptions

three pills. one price. a bag, posted from kyoto.

size, cadence, choice — that's the whole configurator. no bundle tier, no discount ladder. the price updates live as you tap. matches the way Ren wraps a bag at the bar.

copy: "a bag, posted from kyoto. every other sunday." no exclamation marks, no urgency, no countdown timer.

subscriptions are the real revenue — but the design refuses to act like a checkout.
hone.kyoto / #subscribe
hone.kyoto / #classes
04 · 05 / classes

a dark stretch. a different room.

weekday classes are the highest-margin product. the section inverts to ink-black to mark a shift — like walking from the bar to the back room where the cupping happens.

the four scheduled slots are listed plainly with seat counts. "full" is a colour shift, not a disabled button. the cta is bone-white.

a section break that physically resembles a room change in the cafe.
04 · 06 / visit

address in mincho. three house rules.

the address is set in shippori mincho with the romaji underneath — japanese first, latin second. open hours, contact, walking time from demachiyanagi station.

three rules in italic serif: none. ask, if you must. we don't. each with a quiet japanese subline. the rules ARE the welcome.

house rules treated as typography, not warnings. the form of the page is its hospitality.
hone.kyoto / #visit

on a phone, the same quiet.

04 · 07 / responsive

below 960px the hero stacks, the tategaki rail folds away, and the bean rows collapse to a tighter list. nothing else changes — the cream stays cream, the mincho stays mincho.
